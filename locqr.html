Location QR codes

App home

Post these where people work

At login, scanning the QR at a machine or storage location sets where you are. Formats:
PPSPL:LOCATION:M-<factory>-<machine> for a machine and PPSPL:LOCATION:S-<Loc_No> for a storage location (yard, warehouse, WIP Store).
Print on A4 at 100%, margins none. 8 per page.
